Shared objective: decide whether the problem is store availability, shopper awareness, or repeat purchase. Do not treat “listed” as the final definition of success.

Mutual deliverables: the KA confirms pilot outlets, listing dates, stock readiness, and data sharing. The supplier confirms compliant product information, store training, supply cadence, and a budget cap. Name an owner and due date for every item.

Stage gates: start with a bounded group of stores and one review cycle. Agree on measures and a date; scale only when conditions are met. If supply or execution slips, pause and diagnose why.

Value and risk: compare against a shared baseline, including incremental contribution, service cost, and inventory risk; distinguish sell-in from sell-through. Follow applicable laws and company policies—do not put unapproved promotional promises into the plan.
